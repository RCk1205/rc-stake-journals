Introduction to Neural Networks

Published on May 2026 · 5 min read

Neural networks are one of the foundational technologies behind modern artificial intelligence systems. They are designed to simulate the way human brains process information using interconnected layers of computational nodes.

What is a Neural Network?

A neural network is a machine learning model capable of identifying patterns in data. These systems are widely used in image recognition, natural language processing, recommendation systems, and predictive analytics.

Core Components

A standard neural network contains:

  • Input Layer
  • Hidden Layers
  • Output Layer

Each node processes incoming information and passes weighted outputs to the next layer.

Applications

Modern AI systems use neural networks in:

  • Computer Vision
  • Speech Recognition
  • Self-driving Systems
  • AI Chatbots
  • Medical Diagnostics

Conclusion

Neural networks continue to shape the future of intelligent systems. Understanding their fundamentals provides a strong base for deeper AI and machine learning concepts.
